No more chasing anyone for a Bizum.

  • ProjectOwn product · 2026
  • RoleBranding, design and build
  • PlatformMobile web app (PWA)
  • StatusLive at pagopero.com

When someone pays upfront for a group, they use WhatsApp to tell everyone what they owe and to keep track of payments. But messages get lost and lists go out of date.

Pagopero creates a shared payment list that is always up to date. You enter the amount and the names, Pagopero works out what each person owes and generates a link with a ready-to-send message, including the Bizum number.

The conceptual reference was WeTransfer: create, send, done, with no sign-up needed to use it.

The challenge

There are two people with different needs at the same moment:

  • Whoever pays upfront has paid the shared bill and wants it closed without chasing anyone.
  • Whoever checks gets the link, sees how much their share is and which Bizum to pay, and pays outside the app.

And one constraint that shapes everything: Bizum offers no public API for individuals. The app can’t collect or verify payments, so it was designed around human confirmation.

The most important decision

Pagopero doesn’t handle payments. It handles the list you currently have lost in WhatsApp.

WhatsApp messages aren’t updated every time someone pays. The message is the way in, and the list is the up-to-date source of information. That way the work isn’t duplicated.

That’s why everything revolves around a single group link: whoever is collecting shares it once, and the reminder is forwarding that same link, without singling anyone out.

Create and share

What you paid, who owes, and share: the message goes out through WhatsApp, Telegram or wherever you like, and nobody downloads anything. For the MVP, all it takes is names, an amount and a link: asking for phone numbers or emails adds friction without solving the main problem.

Pagopero splits the bill equally, and one person can pay for the people they came with in a single figure («María +3»).

Key decisions

Five decisions explain how Pagopero behaves, beyond the screens.

Late sign-up, and only for whoever collects

Whoever pays never signs up. Whoever collects can save their lists with Google to manage them from any phone, but it’s never mandatory. No password or SMS.

A message that doesn’t demand

After the usability tests, the message was cut down to the essentials: «{Concepto}: son {importe} cada uno. / Bizum al {número} / La lista está aquí 👉 {enlace}» (“{Item}: it’s {amount} each. / Bizum to {number} / The list is here 👉 {link}”). The invitation to use Pagopero was removed: it was redundant, because whoever opens the link already sees «Crea tu lista en Pagopero» (“Create your list on Pagopero”) on the list itself.

Data, always from the network

It’s a PWA: it opens from a link without installing anything. Only the interface is cached; amounts and statuses are always fetched from the network, so nobody sees an outdated figure.

Humor, with care

The brand is playful and the mascot has personality, but the screen for whoever pays is friendly, and the mascot never turns red next to their name. When everything is settled: “This time you paid, but you didn’t treat.”

Measuring without intruding

Our own measurement counts every list without personal data: lists per day, how many get settled, time to first payment and, above all, whether whoever collects comes back. Mixpanel explains how it’s used (screens, errors, where people drop off), but only with consent.

The notice is our own, with «No» and «Sí, vale» (“Yes, fine”) given equal weight, and nothing is downloaded without accepting. On the home page it waits for the intro to finish so it doesn’t cover the first glance.

Outcome

3steps to create a list and share it
0accounts needed by whoever pays
€0goes through Pagopero: payment is by Bizum

Pagopero has been live since 28 October 2026. Usability tests with real people changed specific decisions, such as accessing «Mis listas» (“My lists”) without an account and the length of the message.

Activation, payment rate, time to pay and repeat use are now measured, with our own measurement and Mixpanel with consent.
